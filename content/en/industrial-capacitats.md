# Workshop capabilities

Materials, processes and machinery of the Industrial workshop.

## From material to part

We have a **950 m²** fabrication workshop, with our own welding and installation team. The company has a staff of **12 people**.

## Machinery

We have a **press brake, shear and punching machine**.

## Materials

- Carbon steel
- 304 stainless steel
- 316 stainless steel

## Welding

We work with **MIG/MAG** and **TIG** processes.

## Travel and installation

We have six vans and a crane truck for travel and installation.

## When the project requires technical support

We can quote jobs that require technical architecture support. The project documentation lets us define their scope.

## Improvements in progress · not current certifications

We are implementing ISO 9001 and studying ISO 3834. Digitalisation, automation and process robotisation are also ongoing lines of work.
