# Automation for doors

We install and repair automation for doors and entrances. We work both on motorising a door and on servicing a system that is already installed.

## Let's start by getting to know the door

To work out what needs to be done, it helps us to know three things about the door.

- **Type** · Garage, roller shutter or another entrance.
- **Current condition** · How it works now.
- **Change** · What you want to change.

If possible, attach a photo to your enquiry.
