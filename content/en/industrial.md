## What we fabricate

Fabrication for companies and construction: structures, doors and furniture, in series or made to measure; parts from a drawing or a specification, and single-unit industrial jobs.

- **Structures, railings and staircases** · For builders' and developers' projects.
- **Doors and enclosures** · Fabrication and installation in construction projects.
- **Furniture** · Several units, made to measure for their use.
- **Industrial trolleys** · Adapted to a specific need, as a single unit or in series.

Material, welding, assembly and quantity are assessed together before fabrication.

- **Define** · We review what needs to be fabricated, the material, the units and the available documentation.
