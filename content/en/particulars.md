# Residential metalwork in Vilafranca and the surrounding area

We fabricate and install made-to-measure pieces for homes, residential communities and businesses from our workshop in Vilafranca del Penedès.

## Made-to-measure work. Clear answers

### Doors and motors

We fabricate, install and motorise garage, car park and business doors and entrances.

[See automation](/en/residential/door-automation/)

### Structures

We fabricate and install railings, staircases, walkways and other metal structures made to measure for each space.

[See structures](/en/residential/structures/)

### Furniture

We make furniture with an iron or stainless steel structure, made to measure for its use and its space. We work both for private clients and for interior decorators looking for metal pieces for their projects.

[Ask about your made-to-measure furniture](/en/contact/?tipus=particular&servei=mobiliari)

### Repairs

Doors, roller shutters and motors that have stopped working.

[See repairs](/en/residential/repairs/)

## A series, a technical part or a construction project?

At Industrial we fabricate structures, doors and furniture for series and construction projects, and parts to drawing and industrial trolleys for companies, builders and developers. We also take on single-unit industrial jobs.

[Industrial fabrication](/en/industrial/#produccio)

## Facts, not just words

Three documented jobs in a home, a residential community and a business. The portfolio includes five.

## Close by, with a full workshop

We work mainly in Vilafranca del Penedès and the surrounding area, and we also take on jobs as far as Barcelona. We have our own workshop and vehicles for installation; travel outside our usual area is assessed case by case.
