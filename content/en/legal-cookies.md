## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a file that is downloaded to your computer when you access certain web pages. Cookies allow a web page, among other things, to store and retrieve information about the browsing habits of a user or their device and, depending on the information they contain and the way you use your device, they can be used to recognise the user.

## What types of cookies are there?

There are several types of cookies according to different criteria.

**According to how long they remain active:**

- **Session cookies:** cookies designed to collect and store data while the user accesses a web page.
- **Persistent cookies:** cookies in which the data remain stored on the device and can be accessed and processed for a period defined by the party responsible for the cookie.

**According to who owns them:**

- **First-party cookies:** those belonging to SERRALLERIA CARBÓ.
- **Third-party cookies:** those owned by a third party other than SERRALLERIA CARBÓ, which will process the information collected.

**According to their purpose:**

- **Technical and/or personalisation cookies:** those used to improve the service, locate incidents, recognise the user, etc.
- **Analytics and/or advertising cookies:** those used to analyse browsing information and to show advertising, either generic or personalised.

## Which types of cookies are most common?

- **Technical:** first-party session, identification, configuration and registration cookies, strictly necessary to identify the user and to provide the services they request. The information obtained through these cookies is processed only by SERRALLERIA CARBÓ.
- **Analytics:** cookies which, processed by us or by third parties, allow us to quantify the number of users and thus measure and statistically analyse how users use the service offered. To do so, your browsing on our website is analysed in order to improve the range of products or services we offer you.
- **Advertising:** cookies which, processed by us or by third parties, allow the advertising spaces that may be included on our website to be managed as effectively as possible and/or store information on user behaviour obtained through continuous observation. They make it possible to learn about internet browsing habits and to show advertising related to the browsing profile.

## What is a cookie for?

Cookies are used to offer personalised services and/or advertising, to analyse how the system works, to recognise the user when they access the site, to locate incidents and problems that may arise and solve them as quickly as possible, and to analyse and measure the use and activity of the website.

## Is my consent needed to install a cookie?

Consent is not needed to install technical cookies or cookies that are strictly necessary to provide an information society service expressly requested by the recipient.

For all others, the consent of the data subject is needed. On this website, consent is given when the user, having been informed next to the corresponding button, chooses to load the interactive Google Maps map. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the cookies from your browser, as explained in the section "How do I disable cookies?".

If in the future the website incorporates analytics tools or other services that use non-technical cookies, SERRALLERIA CARBÓ will update this policy and ask for the user's consent, through a settings panel, before installing them.

## How do I disable cookies?

Users can allow, block or delete the cookies installed on their device through their browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Safari for iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
- [Opera](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies)

If third-party cookies are blocked, the interactive map may not work; users can still see the address on the contact page or open it directly in Google Maps.
